Animal voices

Tiny yips, grumbles, chitters and honks for our pixel-art forest.

Adapted from 80 CC0 creature SFX by rubberduck. The original sounds and these adaptations are available under CC0 1.0 Universal. Thank you, rubberduck!

These are performed cartoon creature voices, shortened, pitched and softened for the game. The little squirrel repeats its chitter three times.
